About

Designer & Communications Strategist

I’m a designer and developer passionate about uncovering what matters most to people and bringing it to life through clear strategy, thoughtful design, and reliable technology. I thrive at the intersection of research, experience design, and engineering—moving fluidly from understanding audiences and goals to shaping how something should look, feel, and work.

My experience spans small and large teams, from the agency IBA, Inc. to the marketing team at Sweetwater. I also had the privilege of serving in a leadership role in operations at the Sambodh Center for Human Excellence, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Currently, I run First Pulse full-time, partnering with artists, nonprofits, and mission-driven organizations on brand systems, websites, and campaigns. Recent work includes projects with the Community Foundation of Greater Fort Wayne, Downtown Fort Wayne, Patronicity, and Search Ministries.
