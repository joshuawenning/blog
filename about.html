About

Graphic Designer and Web Developer

I’m a designer who’s passionate about uncovering what matters most to people and bringing it to life across digital and print. I thrive at the intersection of design and engineering—moving fluidly from client research, shaping how an experience should look and feel, and the technical work required to build it.

My experience spans both small and large teams—from the tight-knit agency IBA, Inc. to the marketing team at Sweetwater. I also had the privilege of serving at the Sambodh Center for Human Excellence, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, where I transitioned into a leadership role in operations. Currently, I run First Pulse full-time, blending my love of design and community service with projects and campaigns for clients like the Community Foundation of Greater Fort Wayne, Downtown Fort Wayne, Patronicity, and Search Ministries. When not designing or coding, I’m most likely skateboarding or writing and producing music.
